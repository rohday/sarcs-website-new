About the laboratory

The SARCS Lab is dedicated to advancing the frontiers of computer architecture, hardware-software co-design, and emerging computing paradigms like photonics, quantum computing and in-memory computing. Our research spans RISC-V architectures, hardware accelerators, compute-in-memory, and AI systems, with a focus on developing efficient, scalable, and sustainable computing solutions for next-generation applications.
