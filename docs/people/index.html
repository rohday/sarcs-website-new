Assistant Professor, Principal Investigator

Priyesh Shukla

Prof. Priyesh Shukla leads the SARCS Laboratory at IIIT Hyderabad. His research interests span computer architecture, in-memory computing, hardware accelerators, quantum computing and more. He received his PhD from University of Illinois Chicago and has published in top venues like Nature Communications, TCAS-1, DATE, ICASSP, TVLSI, ISCAS and others.
